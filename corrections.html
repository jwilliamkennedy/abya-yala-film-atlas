Corrections and additions

Suggest a correction

Filmmakers, communities and researchers can correct a record or suggest a filmmaker or film the atlas is missing.

Sending opens a short public note on the atlas’s GitHub page (a free GitHub account is needed), where each correction is reviewed and tracked. Please do not include private information.
