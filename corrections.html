Corrections, withdrawal and participation

Anyone may suggest a correction, an addition or a removal without giving a name, email address or account.

The Atlas is an early-stage prototype. Community consultation and filmmaker-led review are planned, not completed. You can use this form to request a change, restrict a map association, withdraw a record or express interest in future consultation. No submission changes the catalogue automatically.

Privacy: No name, email or account is required. An optional reply email is included only if you enter one. Submissions pass through FormSubmit ↗, a third-party service that may log technical details, so anonymity can’t be guaranteed. Please don’t include private or ceremonial information.
