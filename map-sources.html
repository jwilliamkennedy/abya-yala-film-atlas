Indigenous territories

Native Land Digital, via National Geographic MapMaker. The Atlas uses the distributed territory layer with a source edit date of October 2023, retrieved September 2026; it is not a live copy of Native Land Digital. The globe takes each land’s name and one point from this layer and draws no boundaries. The flat map can show the reference shapes as an optional layer, off by default. Atlas associations are separately recorded as sourced or provisional. They do not establish filming locations or official territorial boundaries.

These boundaries are not legal or statist conceptions of land. They are markers that guide this project’s exploratory ethos, grounded in Native cinema.

Map data provided by Native Land Digital (https://native-land.ca/). Used with permission for educational and non-commercial purposes.

Earth imagery

NASA Blue Marble / GIBS. Blue Marble Next Generation, a cloud-free composite of the Earth at about 500 m, served by NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), part of its Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS). No high-resolution satellite imagery or elevation data is used.

Land, coastline and rivers

Natural Earth. Public-domain vector coastline (1:50 million scale) for the fallback and beneath the imagery, and rivers and lake centerlines (1:50 million scale) at regional scale.

Geographic context

OpenFreeMap · © OpenMapTiles · Data from © OpenStreetMap contributors. Vector tiles supply rivers and lakes at close zoom; no basemap place names are drawn. OpenStreetMap data are available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Rendering and catalogue

MapLibre GL JS renders the globe, which becomes a flat map at regional scale; the fallback uses D3 and Natural Earth. Film credits and artwork are linked from the Atlas catalogue, not the basemap. Image and film provenance are available in each film's sources panel and public catalogue CSV.

Earth imagery depends on NASA GIBS; rivers and lakes at close zoom on OpenFreeMap. Scale and colors are display choices, not evidence of territorial jurisdiction.
