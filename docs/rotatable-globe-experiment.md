# Globe discovery — November 24, 2026

Opt-in beta: `?constellate=1&globe=1`. The previous MapLibre experiment could show an empty sphere. This revision uses the already vendored D3 spherical orthographic projection and a local canvas, with one camera. Natural Earth TopoJSON country geometries are merged, normalized for D3 spherical winding, checked for plausible total land area, and rendered as a continuous coastline. No separate tile camera. At large scales it remains spherical rather than switching to Mercator. This is a renderer decision for the experiment, not a rejection of MapLibre for future work.

The fixed aperture tunes as the globe rotates. A point entering the aperture illuminates; after movement settles, exact spherical polygon containment determines the land references under the camera center. Nearby indexed references are explicitly identified as nearby, not the land at the point. All overlapping existing references remain available, not ranked by film counts. Matching films emerge automatically via the existing land ID and thematic intersection index; deliberate selection opens the existing film detail. No automated camera snapping on filters or film selection, no collection writes. Drag, pinch, wheel, keyboard and explicit land navigation are supported.

## Geography and sovereignty

Reuse the exact existing normalized in-memory reference polygons from the Atlas's Native Land Digital / National Geographic MapMaker 2023 snapshot; no new NLD requests, copies, export, or new geometry files. Existing historical geometry and the current Native Land map are not the same. Source attribution and reference-not-boundary language are shown in the globe footer. Indigenous communities are the stewards of these data. Native Land Digital's current Data Sovereignty Treaty restricts storage or redistribution without explicit permission; current territory API changes and permission for the historical snapshot must be reviewed before public release. See https://api-docs.native-land.ca/data-sovereignty-treaty and https://native-land.ca/about/what-we-map. The experiment does not claim that every geometry or affiliation is verified or authoritative. Existing catalogue associations remain unchanged.

## Acceptance

Browser QA verifies visible land pixels, plausible spherical land area, rotation, fixed aperture, automatic film discovery via an existing land ID, unchanged camera on theme filtering, and no collection mutation. Desktop/mobile tests pending. Draw, personal constellation construction, curated editorial tours and full Watch integration are still separate from this prototype. Keep default beta and production unchanged until tested.
