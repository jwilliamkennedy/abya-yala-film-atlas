# Globe: chosen lands, local land, no satellite (25 November 2026)

John's decision (25 Nov): keep the sphere, drop what made it a surveillance view.

- **No satellite imagery, no elevation.** NASA GIBS, EOxCloudless and Mapterhorn are removed from the globe. Land is the Natural Earth 1:50m coastline (`assets/world-50m.json`, already local) filled in the flat map's stone `#d9dbdd`; the sea is the flat map's blue-grey `#8093a1`; rivers and lakes from OpenFreeMap at zoom 6+, in the same colour. Basemap place, water and peak names stay off, as on the flat map. The Terrain (tilted) button is gone; `syncTerrain` does nothing.
- **Territories at full strength.** Ambient fill 0.30–0.40, edges 0.35–0.70 (Soft/Strong still multiply these). Lands are always on: the Lands switch is hidden and an old `territories=off` link opens with lands shown.
- **No reticle, no tuning.** `#globe-aperture` is hidden (kept in the page because hit-testing measures it). Moving the globe never chooses a land. A land opens when it is chosen: click or tap it, pick it in Find, follow a link, or press Enter (the land at the centre of the view). It stays open while the globe moves, until another is chosen (`heldLand` in `settle()`); Home clears it. With nothing chosen the panel is hidden (`#globe-encounter.is-empty`). Hovering shows the land's name (`#globe-hover-name`).
- **Globe, then flat.** Projection `['interpolate',['linear'],['zoom'],5,'vertical-perspective',7,'mercator']`: a sphere at the scale of the continent, a flat map at the scale of a land.
- **Antimeridian.** The native land fill uses the unmerged countries, with rings made continuous past ±180 and polar rings closed along the pole (`planarRings`). Without this Chukotka and Antarctica drew bands round the globe (previously hidden under the satellite layer).
- **Fallback canvas** uses the same stone, blue-grey and ink; the colour relief raster is no longer drawn.
- `assets/globe-experiment.js` (unused) is deleted.

Checks changed to the new contract: `tools/qa/globe-independent.py` (terrain mode: no remote imagery or mesh, reticle hidden, chosen land held across camera moves; lands always on), `tools/qa/globe-discovery.py`, `tools/qa/globe-share.py`. Local runs (Chromium, SwiftShader, external hosts blocked): terrain, input, discovery-page and share suites passed; the independent discovery mode stops on an artwork-frame check locally (images blocked), as the unedited code also fails locally.

Still to do: Constellate on the globe (night sky, threads as arcs), the theme web as arcs, then retire `flat.html`.

## The film stage; watching in place; thumbnails anchored; the film box (25 Nov, late night)
- **Thumbnail bug fixed.** On the beta every map thumbnail after the first stood 84 px further right than its land, so thumbnails drifted off their nodes and piled on each other. Cause: `.globe-map-thumb{position:relative}` overrode MapLibre's absolute marker, so the markers took up room in the flow. MapLibre now moves a plain anchor (`.globe-map-thumb-anchor`) and the card sits inside it. The same change ends the quarter-second lag of thumbnails behind the map while panning (the card's `transition:transform` had been applied to MapLibre's own positioning) and makes the hover lift work.
- **A film opens in the centre.** A map thumbnail, a Gather square, the artwork in the film box, Play and Watch all open the film stage (`openStage`, the `#globe-player` dialog): artwork (340 px at most, linked to the page it comes from, with its credit), title, filmmaker (to the Atlas record page), recorded affiliation, form, length and languages, Play or the place to watch, Collect, Film page, themes (a theme closes the stage and weaves its lands), land association, Atlas record and Suggest a correction. The land's name and colour are at the top; Previous/Next and the arrow keys step through the land's films, and the film box underneath follows. The picture rises from the thumbnail that was pressed (not with reduced motion). Escape, Close, the land's name or a click outside close it; focus returns to where it came from. The right-hand preview still opens from the film's title.
- **Watching.** Play swaps the artwork for the player in the same place and starts it (the press is the consent; nothing loads from YouTube, Vimeo or the NFB before it). Vimeo is asked not to track (`dnt=1`); YouTube stays on `youtube-nocookie.com`. The NFB's own embed player is now allowed on the globe (`/film/<slug>/embed/player/`), as it already was on the Watch page: 67 films that used to leave for the Watch page now play in place. A film that can only be seen at its source says where before you leave ("Watch at Mirada Nativa (sign-in required) ↗", from the published `watch_1_label`), instead of opening the Watch page on an empty player. Trailers are labelled as trailers. "To watch from <land>" lists up to six more films from the same land that play in place; choosing one while playing keeps playing. The research notes in `watch_*_note` are not shown.
- **Links.** `open=film` is a new state key (`atlas-route.js`): Share and the page address carry it while a film is open, and such a link opens on the film, never playing.
- **Thumbnails.** A small play mark on thumbnails whose film plays in place; the land-name tooltip no longer shows under a thumbnail's caption; a finer border and stem.
- **The film box** (`#globe-encounter`) is centred under the globe when there is room (never under the zoom buttons) and uses the menu panels' family: ink panel, serif land and film names, small capitals, gold for Play and for the current film (an underline, not a green block); the teal and green fills are gone. Long affiliation lines wrap inside their column instead of running under the strip.
- Versions: `globe.js?v=40-stage`, `globe-experiment.css?v=78-stage`, `atlas-route.js?v=6`.
- Checks: `tools/qa/globe-artwork.py` gains FILM_STAGE (Play, arrows, share key, Escape, thumbnails anchored and not overlapping). Local runs (Chromium, SwiftShader, external hosts blocked): route and core unit checks, artwork and discovery suites passed. `tools/qa/globe-share.py` stops at "Connection note" with and without this change.
