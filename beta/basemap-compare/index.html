Territory outlines come from the atlas’s archived 2023 Native Land Digital / National Geographic MapMaker reference and are not definitive Indigenous boundaries or filmmaker locations. The same source geometry is applied to both maps, without modifying it. Clicking an overlaid region lists the mapped source names, not verified affiliations.

The basemaps require no Mapbox token. OpenFreeMap uses OpenStreetMap data. Maptoolkit uses its free community tile service; its attribution and logo remain visible. Neither provider guarantees unlimited uptime. Nothing on this page is being promoted to the production atlas.
