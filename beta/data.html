What is in them

  • Only filmmakers on the public map; research candidates are not included.
  • people_as_recorded is the filmmaker’s affiliation as the atlas records it, from their own words or an explicit biographical source. It does not establish enrolment, citizenship or residence.
  • country_index is the country the atlas files the filmmaker under for browsing.
  • land_references are Native Land Digital / MapMaker (2023) territory references linked on the atlas, not definitive boundaries.
  • A film credited to several filmmakers appears once per filmmaker, with the same film_id where the record shares one.
  • Images are not included; they remain with their rights holders.
