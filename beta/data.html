What is in them

  • Only filmmakers on the public map; research candidates are not included.
  • people_as_recorded is the filmmaker’s affiliation as the atlas records it, from their own words or an explicit biographical source. It does not establish enrolment, citizenship or residence.
  • country_index is the country the atlas files the filmmaker under for browsing.
  • land_references are Native Land Digital / MapMaker (2023) territory references linked on the atlas, not definitive boundaries.
  • A film credited to several filmmakers appears once per filmmaker in atlas-films.csv, and once in the film facets.
  • Images are not included; they remain with their rights holders.

Film facets: languages, themes, form and length

One store holds what the atlas records about each film, one row per film, field and value, each with the page it comes from and how it was recorded. The map’s filters and search, the Languages and Themes pages and these downloads all read from it.

languageA language spoken in the film, as its credits or synopsis state it. value is the atlas’s name for it; code is its Glottolog ↗ code. The language authority adds ISO 639-3 codes and marks each match as exact, group (the credit names a language group, not one variety) or unresolved (the credit does not say which language). Never inferred from the filmmaker’s people or the film’s setting.
themeOne of 30 themes in five families (Land and Environment; Sovereignty and Politics; Knowledge and Continuity; Lives and Relations; Expression and Representation), tagged only from a published synopsis whose link is kept as source_url. code holds the family; the primary theme is marked in basis.
formDocumentary, Fiction, Animation, Experimental, Hybrid or Music Video: from the synopsis where tagged, otherwise normalised from the form recorded with the credit (the original wording is kept in basis).
lengthShort, Mid-length, Feature or Series, recorded the same way as form.
source_url, basisThe page that states the value, and how it entered the atlas.

How the atlas records

  • A filmmaker appears only when their Indigenous affiliation comes from their own words or an explicit biographical source; it is never inferred from a surname, language, subject, setting or festival listing.
  • Every affiliation, film credit and facet keeps its source.
  • Territories are Native Land Digital / MapMaker references, not definitive boundaries.
  • Images stay with their rights holders and are not part of the downloads.
  • Corrections from filmmakers and communities come in through the corrections page.
