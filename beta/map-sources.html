Indigenous territories

Native Land Digital, via National Geographic MapMaker. The Atlas uses the distributed territory layer with a source edit date of October 2023, retrieved September 2026; it is not a live copy of Native Land Digital. The display uses translucent overlapping reference shapes. Atlas associations are separately recorded as sourced or provisional. They do not establish filming locations or official territorial boundaries.

Elevation and relief

Mapterhorn. Elevation tiles provide hillshading and three-dimensional terrain. Source elevations are approximate, not survey measurements. The provider combines multiple open elevation datasets.

Natural Earth. Public-domain vector coastline and shaded-relief raster for the offline overview. Raster: cross-blended hypsometric tints with shaded relief, 1:50 million scale, from the riatelab/geoviz distribution.

OpenFreeMap · © OpenMapTiles · Data from © OpenStreetMap contributors. Vector tiles supply physical and place labels. OpenStreetMap data are available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Rendering and catalogue

MapLibre GL JS renders the tiled globe and terrain; the fallback uses D3 and Natural Earth. Film credits and artwork are linked from the Atlas catalogue, not the basemap. Image and film provenance are available in each film's sources panel and public catalogue CSV.

Imagery and elevation depend on external tile services. Scale, colors and relief are display choices, not evidence of territorial jurisdiction.
