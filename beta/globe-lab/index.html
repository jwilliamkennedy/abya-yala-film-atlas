How to read it

A place-name, a 2023 source polygon, and a filmmaker affiliation are three different records. The globe joins them only through existing Atlas IDs and labeled evidence. Sourced filmmaker links and provisional catalog matches remain separate; country and point-in-polygon are never used to infer identity. Some Indigenous names have no geometry, including historical and political terms. Their inclusion does not imply definitive boundaries.
