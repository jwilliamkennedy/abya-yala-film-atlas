About this map

The country strip counts every catalog entry assigned to a country, including research candidates. In Land, a country opens territorial entries overlapping its reference outline. Country bubbles show the same totals; select one to zoom to its main landmass. Unassigned entries remain searchable. Country associations do not establish citizenship or residence.

Linked cards pair land references with filmmakers. Selecting a filmmaker numbers their land references on the map and in their detail panel. Selecting a land or filmmaker reveals its branches on the map. Relations keeps paired cards by default; curved Relations are available in Layers.

Sage nodes mark land or community references. Groups separate as you zoom. Select a land to reveal its filmmakers; select a filmmaker to reveal linked lands. Solid curves have a retained source; dashed curves are provisional catalog matches. A sourced affiliation still does not prove residence or a territorial claim.

Relations uses the same entries without geographic placement. Names without a mapped extent remain available there. Curves and spacing organize reading; they do not depict distances, descent, or relationships between nations.

These community connections use the existing territorial snapshot. It is separate from Native Land’s language constellation ↗.

Territorial geometry and names: Native Land Digital, distributed by National Geographic MapMaker. Source snapshot dated 2023.

Names may refer to peoples or lands and span different periods. The map does not establish definitive boundaries or filmmakers’ affiliations.
