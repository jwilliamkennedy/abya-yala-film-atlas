Reading the atlas

Larger land names have more linked filmmakers in this index. Indigenous scripts are retained. Connections include sourced affiliations and catalog matches awaiting review.

Preview land by pointing at the map. Select a name to open its filmmakers. Overlapping names appear in the reading panel. Names without a mapped area remain in Land.

Country totals count all catalog entries assigned to each country, independently of filters. They do not establish Indigenous identity or residence.

Solid lines have an affiliation source. Dashed lines are catalog matches awaiting review. The lines connect index references, not filmmakers’ residences.
