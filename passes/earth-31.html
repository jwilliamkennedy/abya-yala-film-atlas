Reading the atlas

Land names grow in size, weight and warmth with the number of distinct linked filmmakers and collectives under the current filters. Counts include sourced affiliations and provisional catalog matches.

Move across the map to read names in the margin. Select a name to explore its indexed filmmakers. Several names may overlap. Names without mapped geometry remain available in Land and in affiliation references.

Country labels and the Countries menu count every catalog entry assigned to that country, including research candidates. Country association does not establish residence, citizenship, or Indigenous identity.

Traces connect source-map reference positions to readable entries. Solid traces have an affiliation source; dashed traces are provisional catalog matches. Neither proves residence or a territorial claim.

Territorial names and geometry: Native Land Digital, distributed by National Geographic MapMaker, 2023 snapshot. Names may refer to peoples, lands, and concepts from different periods. Source geometries are references, not definitive boundaries.
